# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure quality standards are met and acceptance criteria are validated. They collaborate with developers and product leads to define test strategies, execute test plans, and provide quality assurance for releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approach aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute functional, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Validate acceptance criteria before PR approval
- Track and report quality metrics (test coverage, defect density, etc.)
- Coordinate smoke tests and regression testing before release
- Participate in sprint planning to understand testing requirements

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Provide clear quality signals to the team and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement sessions
- QA handoff discussions with developers
- Quality reports and test coverage metrics
- Defect triage and severity discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Work closely during implementation to understand testability requirements; coordinate on test environments and CI integration
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria interpretation and provide quality feedback during feature reviews
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and defect status as part of project health tracking

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies and coaching, helping teams self-organize and remove impediments. They enable effective execution of sprints and iterations.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments for the team
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint backlog and burn-down visibility
- Help teams reflect on process and implement improvements
- Escalate risks and dependencies when needed

### Goals
- Enable high-velocity, predictable delivery
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Maintain team focus and motivation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (daily standup, planning, retro)
- One-on-ones with team members
- Process feedback and coaching

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partner on sprint planning, risk escalation, and team capacity management
- **Developers**: Support through impediment removal and coaching on estimation and process adherence
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and priority discussions with the team

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical strategy, design guidance, and risk mitigation. They collaborate with developers and product leads to make architectural decisions that balance scalability, maintainability, and delivery speed.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and architectural approach
- Review technical designs and PRs for architectural alignment
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Guide technology choices and trade-offs
- Mentor developers on architectural best practices
- Document architectural decisions and rationale (ADRs)

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, scalable architecture
- Reduce technical debt and long-term risk
- Support rapid, confident delivery through clear design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical discussions
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
- Code reviews and mentoring
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design guidance, review implementations for architectural alignment, and mentor on best practices
- **Project Managers**: Identify and communicate technical risks and dependencies early in planning
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Ensure test strategies align with architectural boundaries and integration points

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical delivery. They gather requirements, define acceptance criteria, and ensure solutions address the underlying business problem.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify requirements from stakeholders
- Define clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Document business context and rationale for features
- Validate that solutions meet business objectives
- Assist in risk identification related to business impact
- Communicate trade-offs and prioritization rationale

### Goals
- Ensure solutions solve the right problem
- Minimize rework due to unclear requirements
- Maintain alignment between business objectives and delivery

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements sessions
- Acceptance criteria documentation
- Product backlog refinement
- Business impact analysis and reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on requirements gathering and backlog prioritization
- **Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria during planning and refinement; support in defining testable requirements
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Partner on defining test scenarios and acceptance criteria verification
- **Project Managers**: Communicate requirement changes and business impact of trade-offs
